Dear Falk and Ruth Here are some pictures I had lying around that I never sent you! So I better do so before they get lost. Have sent the title of each or reverse. you have the print of the enclosed negative but am trying to keep my neg. up to date also. The souvenir booklet is rather unusual in that very few of them were printed. The enclosed was given to me by an Australian friend. Jack.
