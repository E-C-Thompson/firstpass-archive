Service Men's Mothers OF ALLERTON NEIGHBORHOOD 2860 BRONX PARK EAST-BB1-BRONX,NY November 23, 1942 Dear Son: I know how interested you are in how our mothers' organization, which we formed some time ago, is getting on. Well here it is. We held an affair to present an Honor Roll of our fighting sons and also to raise money to send Christmas gifts to our boys and to those who are not fortunate enough to have folks at home. The program was good and everyone had a fine time, including your family. The evening was at its best when the Honor Roll was displayed and everyone was anxious to see whose name was on it. And you should have seen how proud your mother was to see your name along with the other boys of our neighborhood. You can pride yourself in the knowledge that your mothers are facing this crisis in a way in which you can be proud. We realize that we cannot fight this war with tears and we are doing all we can to help win the war. You can rest assured nothing will be too hard for us until victory is ours. Sincerely, Mrs Nellie Kuperman Secretary
