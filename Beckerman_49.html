30 SEPTEMBER 1945 DEAR FOLKS AND RUTH: JUST A FEW WORDS TO LET YOU KNOW THAT EVERYTHING HERE B. GOING ALONG OK. HAVEN'T HEARD FROM YOU THIS WEEK * NOR FROM ANYONE ELSE FOR THAT MATTER. CHUBBY HANDLER CAME DOWN TO MANILA ON PASS LAST WEEK AND WE SPENT TWO DAYS TOGETHER. BUT HERE IS SO LITTLE TO DO HERE. RECEIVED A LETTER FROM THE NATURALIZATION SERVICE TELLING ME THAT THEY CAN'T ISSUE ME A CERTIFICATE OF DERIVATIVE CITIZENSHIP AS LONG AS I'M LOCATED OVERSEAS. THEY SAID THE MATTER COULD BE CLOSED AS SOON AS I RETURN TO THE STATES. OF COURSE THAT DOESN'T MAKE IT VERY DEFINATE. BUT THERE ARE A FEW GOOD SIGNS OVER HERE POINTING IN THE DIRECTION OF GOING HOME. THIS CONSISTS MAINLY OF THE FACT THAT THERE ARE MANY OFFICERS ARRIVING FROM THE STATES WITH VERY LOW SCORES. OUR OFFICE HAS ABOUT TWICE AS MANY AS WE NEED. NO LARGE SCALE MOVEMENT HAS STARTED YET? I RECEIVED A COPY OF THE LATEST BULLETIN AND READ OF MANY OF THE OLD GANG GETTING HOME. IMAGINE THAT YOU HAVE HEARD FROM ABE BY NOW. THE WEATHER REMAINS HOT AND WET. IT HASN'T CHANGED VERY MUCH SINCE I ARRIVED. IT'S MY TURN TO WORK THIS SUNDAY * AM SITTING AROUND THE OFFICE READING. IT'S VERY HARD TO WRITE AT THIS STAGE OF THE GAME. WOULD RATHER BE TALKING TO YOU. LOVE AND REGARDS TO ALL [illegible]
