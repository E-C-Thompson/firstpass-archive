Summary
The letter is a heartfelt message from a mother to her son Jack, expressing her love and longing for his return home. She mentions that she is in a ward and that her secretary, Ruthy, is having difficulty writing letters due to language barriers. The mother reflects on the 13 weeks since Jack's departure and her hope for his safe return. She thanks him for a gift and expresses her anticipation for the day when she will see him again. The letter conveys a strong sense of familial love and longing.
