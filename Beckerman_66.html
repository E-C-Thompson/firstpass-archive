Dear Pollie and Ruth: Sorry I haven't written for the last few days. Hope you haven't worn the letter box out. I decided I was going to wait until I heard from you before writing again. I was beginning to feel like I was talking to myself when I wrote you, or talking to a deaf man. Well a few days ago that luck day came and I received about two dozen V mail including several from you and many from my friends. You can't imagine how happy I was to see my name on the face of any envelope again. Just keep on writing. I love it. When any of my friends ask you how I'm getting along don't tell them. Just slap them with my address (which you should always carry around with you) and tell them to find out for themselves. Most of the letters addressed to my present address took between ten and fifteen days in getting to me which is quite good. I've only received V mail so far which seems to be an indication that that is the fastest method. Anyway V mail or air mail, just keep on writing. Could sit down tonight and write you a ten page letter but now that I have all you're questions I'm going to deal with them separately and thus increase my volume of mail to you. First, I heard from Abe [illegible] two letters and of all thing a package from him too. He sent me a set of bars and insignia. Very thoughtful of him and I'm most thankful. They're not very easy to get here. Understand he's at PT Meade. Hope things turn out OK for him. Altho you can't expect too much. Yours too. Thing I wish I'm in and not over than most about some pink. They some more who just worry about him. I'm so glad if you're of you you'd won't be sending of the boy. Me at Dund are they our jobs. Ing with my next letter I shall systematically get around to answering every one of the things you mentioned in your letter. Until then good night. Or Tally-ho. As he say. V--MAIL JACK
