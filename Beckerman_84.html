Dear Paul Ruth: Received another letter from you today so am immediately answering. Yesterday I wrote to you. You came to dated May 7 and still talking about hearing from Abe. But you're not sure that you've heard from him so I won't discuss this any further. Those letters stamps you can see put on by Hq. It doesn't mean anyone has additionally censored my mail. I don't have a stamp of my own, that's all. I've already received Uncle's address and letters to him also to Herber. Received a long letter from Aunt Katie but never have heard from her since although I've written both of them. Glad to hear that the checks have been punctual in arriving please tell me when Abe's checks arrive. Did you get one yet? And don't be silly. Of course I'm not short of cash. In fact I'm even managing as I've told you before, to do some saving. And thanks for the bird. It's a very thoughtful gift. Almost forgot that the 25th is my birthday. I'm 24. My birthday was last Tuesday. And mean that Abe's birthday is the yesterday. Hope we don't have to celebrate many more around here. Love Jack.
