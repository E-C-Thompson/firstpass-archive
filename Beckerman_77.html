Dear Folks and Ruth: Well today was the lucky day. It's probable that a whole boatload of packages arrived from the States today because almost everyone in the office received a package in today's mail. And my lucky share was the camera that you had sent me and that I had been waiting for for so long. I want to thank you very much. It was magnificent of you. How did you ever manage to talk someone out of it. [illegible] you say the price was only $13.00 Why its worth three times that much here. But I could sell it tomorrow for just that. Well anyway I held off buying the camera I told you about. I think this one is really great. One of the boys down here made a camera enthusiast says that it's as good as any I might be able to get here. So I'm going to get to work and gather up a collection of pictures that will really thrill you. Will start this Sunday and tell you how I make out. And I want to congratulate you on the packing. It was really a good job. Could have sent chinaware in it. And I think I already told you that the book came thru also in very good condition. Of course the stationery was just what I needed. But the film-- ah that was the surprise--six rolls. That should last for a while. But if I'm to get a real collection I'll soon be running out. Where did you ever manage to get that many rolls. If any more are obtainable at the same source don't hesitate to gather up a bit of reserve stock for me. And I'll write you when I need more. Today was a luck day in other respects. A whole pile of mail came in, in addition to the packages and I managed to draw nine letters. Boy, just when I had gotten caught up with my mail, lucky for me. Think I'll start double spacing from here as I don't think I'll be able to fill up this page otherwise. Received a card from the Mothers Club. This time I will surely answer them even if I have to sit up nights. I have been writing to a flock of people that I've met in the course of my Army life. People with whom I've become very good friends with, and write to regularly. Also some boys who have gone thru a lot with me., including back to the old Croft days. Received two letters from Abe today. He's been receiving my mail and is getting to be quite a regular correspondent. He's gotten accustomed to his surroundings and is only concerned over you anxiety over him. I try to encourage him as best I can. But he has a very mature attitude towards his position and I know he's fully adjusted himself to everything. It's good to hear him talk like a man. At least his letters impress me that way. I wasn't able to get a wire off for mothers day so will take this opportunity to express my love and appreciation for all you've done for us and hope that you'll soon have us back again. Until then I can only wish that you stand up well and have faith in us. Good night. Jack
