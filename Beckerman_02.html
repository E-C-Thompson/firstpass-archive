Summary
This letter, dated 6 March 1945, is from Jack Beckerman, a U.S. Marine Corps officer, to Mr. G. Youngs of the American Red Cross. Beckerman is seeking information about his friend, Lieutenant Zanger, who was declared "missing in action" on 5 December 1944. He is assisting Lieutenant Zanger's sister, Mrs. Fay Jacobs, in obtaining any available information about his current status.

A.P.O. 923 6 March 1945 Mr. G. Youngs Welfare and Inquiry Service American Red Cross A.P.O. 923 Dear Mr. Youngs: I have been advised by the sister of the following named officer, a combat pilot in the U. S. Marine Corps, that he has been announced as "missing in action", as of 5 Dec. 1944: ZANGER, Noszek, 1st Lt. U. S. Marine Corps VMF 222 MAG 14 c/o F.P.O., San Francisco, Cal. Next of kin to the above mentioned officer is his sister, Mrs. Fay Jacobs, 1190 Pelham Parkway South, New York City. Inasmuch as I am an intimate friend of Lt. Zanger, and am located in his operational Theater, his sister has requested that I assist her in whatever manner possible in obtaining any additional information as to the present status of Lt. Zanger. It would be truly appreciated if any information available to your Office regarding the present status of Lt. Zanger would be furnished to the undersigned. It is understood that the transmittal of any information so furnished is subject to censorship regulations. Thank you, JACK BECKERMANN, 1st Lt., F. D. Office of the Fiscal Dir., USAPPE A.P.O. 923, c/o Postmaster San Francisco, Cal.
