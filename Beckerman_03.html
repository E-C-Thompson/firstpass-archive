Summary
This letter from the Commanding Officer of Marine Fighting Squadron 222 to First Lieutenant Jack Beckerman provides updates on Lieutenant Mosek Zanger's status and details of a flight incident involving Mike, a pilot who was part of a division of four fighters patrolling the Rabaul area of New Britain on December 5, 1944. The letter mentions that Mike's plane was disabled and he had to abandon it, but he was able to parachute safely and was last seen waving to his comrades.

She will, naturally, be apprized immediately by the Navy Department of any further developments. There has been no such additional information. 3. In view of the fact, however, that you state you are an intimate friend of Lieutenant ZANGER, the same report made to his sister is hereby made available to you. "Mike was part of a division of four fighters patrolling the Rabaul area of New Britain, on 5 December, 1944, at 2:35 p.m. The division was circling just west of Keravat airstrip, about 15 miles west of Rabaul town, when Mike and another plane in the division collided in the turn. Neither pilot apparently saw the other. Though his plane was disabled and had to be abandoned he had ample altitude and was able to jump easily. The remaining planes then circled close to his chute and he was seen to wave to them, indicating that he was unhurt. The parachute landed about three miles west of Keravat airstrip.
