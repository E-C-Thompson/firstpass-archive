--- Page 1 (Beckerman_79a) --- Dear Folks and Ruth: The week has been pretty bad this week. Very little has come out the office for anyone. I haven't gotten any letter but since it is almost a week since I have last wrote you, thus I'd better drop these few lines to let you know that everything is still going along in fine shape. Today is Sunday and it has been beautiful. Probably like the weather you must be having at home right now. I'm having a little trouble with the camera adjusting the lens for the right amount of light, distance, etc. One of the boys tells me that Kodak puts out a chart telling just what speed and distance to have the lens set at. He said you can find it up at most camera stores. Would you see if you could get me one. This info may come in handy - "No. 2A Kodak Cartridge Hawk-eye Model B lens". I had a different one way back. I was in all Kodak for so long. --- Page 2 (Beckerman_79b) --- 1944 rent 2. King & a
