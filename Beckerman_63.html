--- Page 1 (Beckerman_63a) --- Dear Parents: Ruth. This is just to keep you posted that everything is O.K. I only hope the delays that are bound to occur on your mail may not last too long. I thank God for air mail getting the quickest service. I haven't gotten any mail since the beginning of December so you can imagine how I feel. I have become accustomed to being in an office and am most pleased about my new job. There is lots to learn the same things are done very differently here. My luggage still has not caught up with me until we weren't all moved to take more than 50 pounds aboard the plane. Had to buy a whole lot of new clothes to wear in the meantime. Our going to send you a check for about $100 at the end of this month when I get paid. Have you received any other checks? How is everyone? I am enjoying things immensely - especially the weather which is terrible. Do you think you had better send me some books that I can read. I need regret now not bringing them with me. Well off to bed now. The nights are beautifully cool. Good night. Love. Jack. --- Page 2 (Beckerman_63b) --- [illegible]
