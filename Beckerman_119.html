UNITED STATES NAVY ARMED GUARD DETACHMENT M.S. DONA NATI 7 JANUARY, 1944 THIS IS TO CERTIFY THAT THE BEARER JACK BECKERMAN HAS THRU HIS FINE SPIRIT OF COOPERATION AND DEVOTION TO DUTY CONTRIBUTED IN GREAT MEASURE TO THE SAFE PASSAGE OF THE M.S. DONA NATI THRU ENEMY INFESTED WATERS, AND DELIVERED A VITAL CARGO OF MEN AND EQUIPMENT TO THE FIGHTING FRONT. HE IS ENTITLED TO DRAW ANY AND ALL BONUSES USUALLY ISSUED TO ALL NAVAL PERSONNEL UPON SAFE ARRIVAL, FROM ANY NAVAL DISBURSING OFFICER SO INCLINED TO HONOR THIS CERTIFICATE. ANY NAVAL ACTIVITY DESIRING A VOLUNTEER FOR HAZARDOUS DUTIES AFLOAT MAY COMMUNICATE WITH THIS COMMAND FOR MORE COMPLETE DETAILS CONCERNING THE BEARER'S OUTSTANDING QUALIFICATIONS. Charles H Dana SEAL CHARLES H. DANA LT.(J.G.) USNR COMMANDING OFFICER
