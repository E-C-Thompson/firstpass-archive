Dear Parents Ruth: Just scribbling these few words before I begin work this morning to let you know all is O.K. Haven't received any mail at all during the past week. Managed to pick up something nice for Ruth - will send it soon. I have been picking up odds and ends during my lunch hour in some of the large department stores. As soon as accumulate a pile I send it home for you to keep for me. There are several books on Australian life and art as well as several articles of leather. There are quite plentiful. Tell me if there anything in leather you particularly want. Another thing very attractive here is the precious stone you can buy.
