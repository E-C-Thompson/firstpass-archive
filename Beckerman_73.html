Dear Folks and Ruth: Just received my first letter from Abe a few minutes ago and am sitting down immediately to write you of it. Just in case that this letter reaches you before his does. I imagine the that you've heard from him by now. If you haven't however I think I better tell you now that he has landed in New Guinea. His letter seems to have been written in fairly high spirits. The date was censored out so X don't know exactly when he arrived here but it couldn't have been very long ago. He says that he received your mail between the dates of Feb 6 and Mar 25th for which he seems to be very thankful. I have been writing to him as I've told you to all the addresses that I have been receiving from him. however he hasn't received any. I think he's getting along very well. Just thought that perhaps I should have sent you his letter by air mail but it's just as well telling you about it. That's all about what he has written. Mail between us should get just a few days service so I can give you my word that we'll maintain pretty close contact with each other. I know it will be a big help to him. And you write him often too I hope. New Guinea is quite a different place now than it was when the first operations were in progress there. He shouldn't have too bad a time of it. Boy I'm looking forward to running in to him and you can bet that before this things over you're going to have a couple of photographs of both of us in this part of the world. Let me know how you feel about this. Am going to write him as soon as I finish this. They usually don't keep a man up there for more than six or seven months so you needn't worry about him spending the rest of his time up there. I'll be able to find out exactly where he's located at but naturally I won't be able to write you of it. Other than that there really isn't any thing I definitely had in mind written you about. Received another Bulletin from the Mo hers club which you had already sent me. Also some newspapers from City College. None of the packages have arrived as yet. The boys here are having a party tonight so I think I'll stop typing this now and run along to get some supper. Besides I'm getting out of material. Will continue in the morning and then mail this off to you. Hope you are still taking the trouble of keeping in touch with me because lately I've received very little mail from you. However I guess you're are quite pre-concerned about Abe. But now that he's arrived I hope that things will get a bit back to normal. Well here it is the following morning. I nearly froze last night. It's getting mighty cold here in the early mornings. I have just finished composing a pile of [illegible] to him. I'll buy some more. So I'm not [illegible] to him or anything. And don't [illegible] him too much. I love you. Sincerely yours, [illegible]
