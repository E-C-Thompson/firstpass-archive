FirstPass — Document Archive

E.C. Thompson  ·  Texts & Technology PhD candidate

This site provides access to digitized primary source materials processed using FirstPass — a locally-run, privacy-preserving LLM pipeline for archival document processing, developed by E.C. Thompson as part of dissertation research in the Texts & Technology program. Metadata and transcriptions are produced by a locally-run large language model and require expert verification before use in research or publication.

Collections

Beckerman Family Collection Live

WWII correspondence, 1941–1945  ·  116 items processed  ·  Letters, photographs, postcards

Wartime correspondence between Captain Jack Beckerman (U.S. Army, USAFFE, Pacific Theater) and his family in New York, and between his brother Abe (Camp Croft, South Carolina) and Jack. The collection includes personal letters, photographs, and documents from the Pacific Theater. Processed with permission of the Beckerman family.
