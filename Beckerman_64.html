Hope you get my letter before the three months are up - that is connection with the Red Cross inquiry you told me about. Now for some of your questions. Please take me nice since it made it easier for me to write. First we live at the edge of town in what was formerly a park. There no rules taking us to walk in the morning. They run on a regular schedule all day. Just a 15 min. ride. So trump is no problem. When there are no buses we ride the train which takes a little longer. There are very crowded. Just as the IRT in such bus. There is no regular officer mess except at the officers club in town. There are two of them that serve excellent meals and we eat there most often. There are many restaurants which we also go to. Usually eat breakfast in a depot store. As I've already told you prices are very reasonable and the food is nothing short of the best. I'm going to straighten and account out as soon as the. Needs me all the pertinent information. We handle allotment right in our office. I'm going to continue writing to Vina them help'd in.
