Dear Folks! Please excuse this hurried letter. I'm just springing it on account the other thing I have to do. I don't want you to feel that I'm neglecting writing. I have about 15 letters to answer but just haven't the chance to get at them. I have been terribly busy during the last week and probably will be so until about July 10th. So if you don't hear from me as often as you'd expect please don't worry about it. I'm at the same job and things are still going very well. But I don't think I'll have much time to spare for letters.
