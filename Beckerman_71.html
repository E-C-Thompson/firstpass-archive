Dear Parents, Ruth: Am spending the evening answering some of my music which I have unfortunately allowed to accumulate to an excess. Also am concerned over not hearing you for some time. Also nothing from Abe. Heard from a buddy who says he's learning. Has Mom written yet? Received another letter from Pearl Chapnick who you must thank for being so faithful in answering all my letters so promptly. Spent this past weekend at the beach again: Still hoping very much occupied; Hope you do take the trouble of writing again soon. Don't see any reason outside of delayed mail for this silence. Haven't received any shortage. I'm not sure if I do under take part only was well and you are love regards Sach
