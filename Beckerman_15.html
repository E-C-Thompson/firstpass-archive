Dear Falls & Ruth: Brisbane 1 March 1945 as I suspected I re'd your letter just a day after I wrote last. So this is a kind of supplement to my last letter answering your. Thanks for explaining the Clintones and greetings from Fortham. Very thoughtful. Notice your letter full of speculations about fall of Berlin etc. It's long overdue now. The news today is indeed refreshing and makes horsegory almost a likely reality rather than a cliched dream. Have noted Abe's organization in the news dispatches which constantly refer to the "XI Corps Sector". Guess it is very easy for you to follow him these days. I had a letter from him a little while ago and everything seems to be O.K. which reminds me to tell you (Ruth) that there were a few things mentioned in your letter that could have been better omitted. Remember I'm in the Army and not a civilian. First about moving. And then about the Wallace debate. Think you know what I mean. I'm not too interested in going into it. I'm having a pretty good time off tail. A fine gay Washington rain. Are beginning to be more frequent. How's everything at the old Homestead. Love & Regards. Jacob
