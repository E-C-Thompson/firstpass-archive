The De La Rama Steamship Co., Inc. Date DECEMBER 25TH, 1943 M.S. DOÑA NATI FROM HIS MAJESTY, THE KING OF ENGLAND. TO ALL SEAMAN OF THE MERCHANT NAVY. SUBJECT A GRACICUS MESSAGE. BUCKINGHAM PALACE ON BEHALF OF MY PEOPLES THROUGHOUT THE WORLD I SEND CHRISTMAS GREETINGS TO ALL THE SEAMAN OF THE MERCHANT NAVY AND THE FISHING FLEETS AND ESPECIALLY TO THOSE WHO MUST SPEND CHRISTMAS AWAY FROM HOME. ON THIS FIFTH CHRISTMAS DAY OF THE WAR WE CAN LISTEN WITH AN EVER GROWING HOPE TO ITS MESSAGE OF PEACE ON EARTH EVEN THOUGH WE CANNOT FORGET THAT THERE IS STILL MUCH TO BE ACCOMPLISHED - MUCH TO BE ENDURED BEFORE THAT MESSAGE CAN BE FULFILLED. MEAN- WHILE THE THOUGHTS OF ALL OF US TURN WITH GRATITUDE TO THE MEN WHO STOOD STEADFAST THROUGH THE DARKEST DAYS - WHO NEVER FAL- TERED EVEN WHEN THE ENEMY'S ATTACK ON OUR SHIPS WERE AT THEIR FIERCEST AND WHO HAVE PLAYED SO GREAT A PART IN THE SUCCESS ACHIEVED BY OUR FIGHTING FORCES IN ALL THEIR OVERSEAS THEATRE OF WAR. WITHOUT YOUR DEVOTED SERVICE THERE COULD BE NO VICTORY FOR OUR ARMY. FROM THE MASTER IN COMMAND TO THE BOY OF HIS FIRST VOYAGE YOU HAVE WORKED TOGETHER WITH THE STEADY DISCIPLINE OF FREE MEN WHO KNOW WHAT IS AT STAKE. YOUR REWARD IS THE CON- CIOUSNESS OF DUTY DONE AND THE AFFECTION AND RESPECT OF ALL YOUR COUNTRYMEN. I SEND YOU EVERY GOOD WISH FOR A SAFE VOYAGE AND A HAPPY LANDFALL. GEORGE VI
