Dear Folks: JUST A FEW WORDS TODAY. CHRISTMAS AND THE THERMOMETER IS OVER 100 DEGREES. I'VE GOT A LITTLE SOUVENIR OF THE PARTY THAT THE MEN GAVE LAST NIGHT SO I THOUGHT THAT IT WOULD BE BEST TO SEND IT RIGHT ALONG TO YOU WHILE I STILL HAVE IT IN MIND. THE ENLISTED MEN IN OUR OFFICE HAVE THEIR OWN CLUB DOWN TOWN AND IT'S REALLY NICE. PICTURES OF IT ARE CONTAINED IN THE CENTER SPREAD OF THE ATTACHED SOUVENIR. WE GET TO GO DOWN THERE ONLY ON THE OCCASION OF AN OFFICIAL PARTY. AND THEY MANAGE TO HAVE THEM QUITE REGULARLY. LAST NIGHT'S AFFAIR MUST HAVE BEEN QUITE HECTIC BECAUSE NONE OF THE BOYS WERE IN ANY SHAPE TO WORK THIS MORNING AND THEY WERE ALL LET OFF AT THREE OCLOCK. ALL THE OFFICERS CONTRIBUTED A PORTION OF THEIR LIQUOR RATION TO THE CLUB AND SO THEY MANAGED TO ACCUMULATE QUITE A LARGE STOCK. OFFICERS ARE ENTITLED TO A MONTHLY RATION OF AMERICAN LIQUORS WHICH IS CONSIDERED ALMOST GOLD IN AUSTRALIA. THE MEN DON'T DRAW THAT RATION AND SO THIS IS THE FIRST TASTE MANY OF THEM HAVE HAD IN A LONG. NO LONG FACES FROM MOM PLEASE. I AM NOT TURNING INTO A DRUNKARD. WE HAD A PARTY OF OUR OWN LAST NIGHT AT THE HOME OF ONE OF THE GIRLS THAT WORKS IN OUR OFFICE. IT WAS QUITE THE AFFAIR AND EVEN MADE THE "SOCIETY PAGE" OF THE BRISBANE NEWSPAPER. NATURALLY THERE WAS PLENTY OF EAT AND DRINK AND A SORT OF LAWN PARLOR FOR DANCING.....THIS AFTERNOON IT WAS SO HOT THAT ALL THE BEAUTIFUL DINNERS THAT EVERYONE WAS PREPARING WAS GOING FOR NAUGHT. IT WAS JUST TOO WARM TO GET LOADED WITH FOOD. SOMEONE PULLED A PRIZE JOKE LAST NIGHT BY PAINTING A SIGN AT THE PARTY "PLEASE BRUSH THE SNOW OFF YOUR SHOES BEFORE ENTERING!!". I RECEIVED A LETTER FROM ABE YESTERDAY AND HIS NEWS IS REALLY ENCOURAGING. HE SEEMS SO FEEL THAT HE IS REALLY IN A GOOD SPOT NOW. HE SENT ME THE CLIPPING YOU HAD FORWARDED CONTAINING THE SKETCH OF DAVE PINSKY. IT WAS A MOST PLEASANT SURPRISE TO GET HOLD OF THAT AND WILL MAKE GOOD USE OF IT BY SENDING IT TO SOME OF THE OTHER *VS AROUND WHO KNOW WILL HAVIT QUIT YOU ARE ING WELL NOW WE THINKING TO FELLOD HERE WDAVE. E TO CATS FOR NO HOPE ALL FEEL AND I KE ALL THE SAM.* * WILL STMAS OFUS ALL TAGAIN? I
