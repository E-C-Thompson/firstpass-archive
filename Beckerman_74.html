--- Page 1 (Beckerman_74a) --- continued - I was very glad to learn about the camera you sent. As long as there's 6 packs of film I'm all set. I won't bother getting another. But keep on sending film. It's unobtainable here. Haven't worked any Sundays since I wrote you about that new deal. Usually only when we think we have an exceptionally large work load. Glad Momma is taking things a bit easier. Haven't sent Ruth's gifts to her. The jewelry is still making on it. Wait until she sees it. I think it is really nice and very unusual. Didn't realize she's out of high school already. I guess she just started a short time ago. I guess everything has kept on moving while I've been away. It's even hard for me to think that she is now in New Bern. It seems like just left for the army when I was on my last furlough. Roy Young's really smart. Has Ruth made up her mind as to what she's going to study at college. I'm very interested in knowing. Hope it's something more intellectual than business or art. And talking about which college to go. There are others besides City or Hunter. I would like to know what you think. I'm not so sure now myself. Very hard to say. There are a lot of advantages you know that City or Hunter can't offer. Think I'll continue in another sheet. --- Page 2 (Beckerman_74b) --- She read carefully what you It is something that has to be decided soon; and we did consider the possibilities of a part college such as N.Y. On One. There is no question as to whether I'd want to go there; I definitely would, & there is only one setback: the expense; getting down to count figures it would cost approx. at most 200 for a full 4 yr. course, but according to our circumstances it doesn't seem that will be too much to undertake. If Papa is in proper state & thinks the cares are in a need so the answer will be in a different light.
