Dear [illegible], Once again, have to apologize for not writing more often. Am keeping well occupied however. The mail really came in this past week and I will have to spend the next few evenings one - writing letters. Received your typewriter. Small of the 24th. Wish you'd take your V-mail from now on. It's very helpful and get much a page. The letter is most important. Glad to know Abe's address. We'll find out. So you what it is. With New Orleans post office it might be So. America. Received a lot of Christmas cards last week. One from CCNY, [illegible], Mrs. Fania. (Thank you. In me, once you?) Also a package of chocolates for the [illegible] club. Dated 17 Dec. (which wasn't exactly fit for eating.) Haven't bought that camera, nor have I received those books you sent. Understand they're starting to see cigarette lighters in the States. If you can get a good one please do so. It's quite hard to get [illegible] here. Spend the weekend at another beautiful off-road club on the sea shore, some distance from here. It was beautiful. Went horseback riding. Had only you by without much trouble. The last night was the place that is housed in [illegible] was an most expensive hotel and going up into the mountains this coming weekend. Hope you are all well — [illegible].
