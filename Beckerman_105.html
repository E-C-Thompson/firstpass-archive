--- Page 1 (Beckerman_105a) --- Dear Jack, As you know I have been attending N.Y.A. school and learning welding. I took gas welding for 2 weeks and electric welding for 8 weeks. Most of the welding jobs the school got was out of town and I didn't want to leave N.Y. I finally got a job working in a shipyard at City Island where they make landing barges. I work from 8 to 5:30, which is 9 hours + 4 hours Saturday. The salary is 36 dollars. Today it was so cold that they closed the yard for the day because when you weld on such cold metal the weld cracks. I have a chance to make more after a while. The job is not far away to travel and I like to work in the shipyard very much. We spend half the time keeping warm and I --- Page 2 (Beckerman_105b) --- don't work hard, Do [illegible] Abe
