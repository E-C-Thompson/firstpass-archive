Dear Falko and Ruth: Another week and another letter. Received a letter yesterday from Alex. He asked me to send the enclosed pictures to you. We made an arrangement whereby each of us would send the photos to you then the other. So I'm sending him some photos today which he'll forward on to you. He really is looking good. Isn't he. And back in civilization again. Too bad he never did get a chance to come down here. I'm sending some packages some of things I've accumulated and don't need. Really not in mood for writing tonight. Everything going well. I'm back. So you'll know. My plenty of happy are a thought. Jack.
