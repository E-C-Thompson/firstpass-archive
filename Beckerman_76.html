Dear Parents: Received a V-mail from you today. The typing was very clear and I'm glad your using it. Already sent it to Abe so don't have it in front of me. I have been sending him your letters and writing to him regularly. Read one from Luni today. You told about being relieved of all that pressure upon hearing from him. I'm glad of that. Thanks very much for sending Sam's address. I wrote Luni tonight. I'm waiting for that new Bulletin you wrote about. Would you ask Mrs. Kuperman to send one to Murray Sanger. He writes me that he hasn't been receiving any. If you see Idea's mother, tell her I'll write Luni as soon as possible and to send me Luni's address. No doubt you are still worrying about Abe. You must try not to since it's making it harder for him. Every one down here has been up to New Guinea and it's just a matter of taking care of yourself until you sent out. You must get accustomed to the idea because there is no alternative. Saw the WAC's arrive here today. The first contingent. They sure look good. Sent Ruth's present out today and you should be receiving shortly. Received the letter and I'm glad about it and sent what I had to them. Good-bye. Tom
